# Off-Page SEO Plan: The World of Packaging
Manual work (outside the website). White-hat only: no bought links, PBNs, link farms, spun guest posts or fake reviews — these get sites penalised.

## Month 1 — Foundation (entity + citations)
Goal: Google and AI assistants recognise the business as a real, consistent entity.
1. **NAP consistency**: one exact Name, Address, Phone/WhatsApp, website, used everywhere below. Name: "The World of Packaging"; mention "formerly Anmol Printers" in descriptions.
2. **Google Business Profile** (factory address): category "Packaging company" + "Printing service" + "Graphic designer"; services list; 20+ real photos (factory, machines, boxes); weekly posts.
3. **Bing Places** (copy from Google).
4. **Social profiles** (complete bio, link, logo, cover, first 9 posts): LinkedIn company page, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest (very strong for packaging), Behance, Dribbble, YouTube (factory/process shorts), X.
5. **B2B / manufacturer directories**: Europages, Kompass, Alibaba (supplier profile, optional), Made-in-China not needed, Global Sources (optional), TradeIndia/TradeKey, Pakistan directories (Pakistan Chamber listings, Lahore/Karachi Chamber of Commerce member directory if member, PakBiz, BizPakistan).
6. **Agency directories** (design side): Clutch, DesignRush, Sortlist, GoodFirms, Upcity, The Manifest — complete profiles, service focus "Packaging design", portfolio.
7. **Packaging-specific listings**: PackagingConnections, Packaging Gateway supplier directory, PackagingDigest supplier guide, Packaging Europe supplier listings (where free/allowed).

## Month 2–3 — Authority links
1. **Design showcase submissions** (high-authority, packaging-relevant): The Dieline, Packaging of the World, World Brand Design Society, Lovely Package, Behance curated galleries. Submit only our own real projects with client permission.
2. **Guest articles** (2 per month) on e-commerce, Shopify, Amazon-seller, cosmetics-startup and branding blogs: useful topics ("How to choose rigid vs folding carton", "Packaging cost guide for startups"). One natural link to a relevant guide or service page.
3. **Expert quotes / digital PR**: answer journalist requests on Qwoted, Featured.com, SourceBottle, Help a B2B Writer — packaging, sustainability, export, e-commerce topics.
4. **Linkable assets** (we publish, others link): packaging glossary, cost guide, box size calculator/dieline templates, labelling requirement guides by country, sustainability guide. Outreach to bloggers/universities/resource pages that list such tools.
5. **Supplier & partner links**: paper/board suppliers, ink suppliers, machinery brands, shipping/freight partners, clients (with permission) — "our packaging partner" mentions or case studies.
6. **Trade shows & associations**: exhibitor listings (e.g. Packaging Expo / ProPak / local print expos), Pakistan packaging and printing associations, export promotion bodies (TDAP) — member/exhibitor pages link back.
7. **Unlinked mentions**: set Google Alerts for "The World of Packaging" and "Anmol Printers"; ask sites that mention us to add a link.

## Ongoing — Reviews & community
1. **Reviews**: ask every completed client for a Google review (and Clutch/Trustpilot for international clients). Reply to all reviews. Never write or buy reviews.
2. **Q&A presence**: helpful answers on Quora, Reddit (r/packaging, r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, r/AmazonSeller), Shopify Community — link only when truly relevant, mostly no link.
3. **Pinterest**: pin every portfolio image with keyword-rich titles/descriptions linking to the matching service page.
4. **YouTube/Shorts**: factory process, rigid box making, collapsible box assembly, unboxing — link in descriptions.
5. **LinkedIn**: weekly posts from the company page and founder (projects, factory, export tips).

## Monthly targets (realistic)
- 10–15 new citations/profiles (Month 1), then 4–8 quality referring domains per month.
- 2 guest articles, 4–6 PR/quote pitches, 1 showcase submission.
- 5+ new Google reviews.

## Tracking (spreadsheet columns)
date · site · type (citation/guest/PR/showcase/directory/review) · URL · target page · anchor text · status · DA/DR · follow/nofollow · notes.
Anchor text mix: ~50% brand ("The World of Packaging"), ~25% URL/generic ("this guide", "website"), ~25% partial keyword ("rigid box manufacturer", "cosmetic packaging design"). Avoid repeating exact-match anchors.

## Tools
Google Search Console (links report), Bing Webmaster Tools, Ahrefs Webmaster Tools (free for own site), Google Alerts.
