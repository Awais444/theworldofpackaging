# SEO Content Plan: The World of Packaging
Source: awaiskhadim.com Google Search Console export (last 3 months, ~11,200 impressions, 206 clicks).
Use this as a keyword map only. **Write 100% original content. Do not copy or paraphrase awaiskhadim.com pages.**

## What the GSC data shows
- Their packaging traffic comes from three page types:
  1. **Compliance / requirements guides** (biggest): UK labelling requirements (424 impr), supplement label FDA guide (375), net weight requirements (212), alcohol TTB labels (207), US food label requirements (133), barcode UPC/EAN (122).
  2. **Service pages**: cosmetic packaging design (pos 8.8), luxury packaging design (13.8), flexible pouch design, label design, food packaging, bottle label, box packaging, Amazon A+/listing images, product mockups, packaging brand strategy.
  3. **"Top packaging designers in [country]" and "packaging designers [city]" posts**: ~80 posts, each 5–80 impressions.
- Top non-brand queries: "packaging design services" (238 impr, pos 24), "dietary supplement label design" (127), "packaging design agency" (110), "supplement label design" (61, pos 8.6), "luxury cosmetic packaging" (pos 5.4), "supplement facts label" (pos 7.3), "skincare manufacturer packaging design services" (pos 2).
- Volumes are small and almost no clicks yet: the opportunity is open, not owned.

## Priority 4: Location content (only with real, specific content)
Awais has ~80 "top packaging designers in [country/city]" posts; each gets few impressions and they are thin. Our approach: **fewer, stronger pages**.
- Keep the 6 ship-to pages; expand to: Singapore, Ireland, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Bahrain, Malaysia, Netherlands, South Africa, Sweden, New Zealand, Pakistan (these appeared in GSC).
- Each page: local labelling rules, shipping times and method from our factory, currency, popular box styles, relevant case study, FAQ. No invented "top 10 designers" lists.
- Optional city pages only for major markets (London, Dubai, New York, Toronto) with genuinely local content.

## Priority 5: Case studies (/work/...)
Awais's case study pages rank for product-specific terms (pouch, hot pot box, serum, supplement). Each real project we publish should be named by product + format, e.g. "Ashwagandha supplement stand-up pouch design", "Vitamin serum rigid box".

## On-page rules for every page
- Title: "{Primary keyword} | The World of Packaging" (≤60 chars). Meta description ≤155 chars with a benefit + "own factory" + CTA.
- H1 contains the primary keyword; supporting keywords used naturally in H2s and FAQs.
- 900–1,500 words on service pages, 1,500–2,500 on guides.
- Internal links: service ↔ related guides ↔ matching box styles ↔ industry page.
- Schema: Service + FAQPage (services), Article + FAQPage (guides), BreadcrumbList everywhere.
- Every guide links to 2 service pages and the quote form; every service page links to 3 guides.
