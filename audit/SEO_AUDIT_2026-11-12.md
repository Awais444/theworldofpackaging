# Site-wide SEO / AEO / GEO audit: 12 Nov 2026

Production build of `main`, plus the live domain (theworldofpackaging.com) and the Vercel deployment.
Tools: `scripts/seo-crawl.mjs` (367 URLs), `scripts/validate-schema.mjs` (225 pages), `scripts/check-images.mjs`, Lighthouse 12.2.1 mobile.

## Healthy

- **Crawl:** 367 URLs, all 200. 0 broken links, 0 redirect chains, 0 orphan pages.
- **Titles and meta:** 0 duplicate titles and 0 duplicate meta descriptions. Every indexable page has one H1, a canonical and breadcrumbs.
- **Structured data:** 0 errors across 225 pages.
  - Types: Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList (224), FAQPage (196 pages, 1,162 Q&As), Service (112), Product (17), BlogPosting (46, all with author), HowTo, CollectionPage, DefinedTermSet.
- **Images:** 178 measured, none oversized or over budget.
- **AI crawlers:** robots.txt allows GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and Applebot.
- **llms.txt:** `/llms.txt` (1,361 words) and `/llms-full.txt` (5,606 words) carry the correct facts and the new email.
- **Sitemap:** a sitemap index with 6 sections plus an image sitemap.
- **Lighthouse SEO:** 100 on every page tested.

## Fixed in this pass

1. **`/glossary` title:** it was 74 characters. It is now "Packaging Glossary: Terms Explained".
2. **OG images:** added to `/team`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/inserts`, `/glossary` and `/countries`.
3. **Case study schema:** `/work/[slug]` now has CreativeWork structured data (it had only breadcrumbs).
4. **Homepage key-facts strip:** its `<dl>` held elements a `<dl>` may not contain. It is now a plain list. Accessibility on the homepage went from 91 to 97.
5. **"How it works" step numbers:** they were silver-300 at 1.58:1 contrast. They are now ink-400, at about 3.4:1 (AA for large text).
