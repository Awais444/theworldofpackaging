## Long description (about 160 words, Clutch / DesignRush "About")

> The World of Packaging is a packaging design studio and printing press based in Toronto, Ontario, with 15+ years in packaging. We work with founders and brand managers in cosmetics, skincare, food, coffee, supplements, candles and apparel, from first launches to full product ranges.
>
> Our services cover packaging design, label design, structural and dieline design, 3D mockups and brand identity. On the production side we make custom rigid boxes (magnetic closure, lid and base, drawer, book style and collapsible rigid boxes that ship flat) and print folding cartons, mailer boxes, labels and stand-up pouches.
>
> Because design and printing happen under one roof, what you approve on screen is what comes off the press. Every order includes free design support, a digital proof and a 3D mockup before print. Minimum order for rigid boxes is 500 units. We design bilingual English and French packaging for the Canadian market and deliver across Canada and worldwide by air or sea.
