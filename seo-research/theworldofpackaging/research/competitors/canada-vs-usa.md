**Canada takeaways**
1. One templated competitor owns the Canada results. Its pages are thin and repetitive, which is beatable with real, specific pages.
2. Nobody in Canada combines a design studio + rigid/collapsible manufacturing on one site. That is our position.
3. Directories (Clutch Canada/Ontario/Toronto, DesignRush Toronto, YP.ca, ensun.io) appear for every "design company" query. A profile on each is a quick win.
4. Buyer guides rank ("best custom packaging companies Canada" by KimEcopak; PackMojo's Toronto guide). We can publish an honest Canada buying guide.

**USA takeaways**
1. Far more crowded than Canada, with many look-alike "custom boxes" sites. Hard to win head terms quickly.
2. Winnable: specific rigid/collapsible pages that are better than QinPrinting's (it already sets the bar: FAQ + Product schema + MOQ shown).
3. Competitors earn links through paid press releases and guest posts (barchart, financialcontent, pctechmag, programminginsider). Our knowledge base flags this as `[RISK]`; don't copy it.

## Our pages vs the benchmark

Our box pages already have FAQ (5), Product + FAQPage + Breadcrumb schema, spec table, related boxes and a stated MOQ, so structurally we match QinPrinting and beat PakFactory. Gaps to check next: testimonials (we have none real), price signals, and Canada-specific copy on box pages.

## Recommended focus

1. **Canada first:** box-style pages + `/countries/canada` with Toronto/Canada wording, a Canada buying guide, Clutch/DesignRush/YP.ca profiles, Google Business Profile.
2. **USA second:** collapsible and magnetic rigid pages as the spearhead (low competition quality), then cosmetics/candles/supplements industry pages.
3. Skip head-on competition for mailers, labels and pouches at low MOQ (web-to-print wins there); position them as "designed + printed" bundles instead.
