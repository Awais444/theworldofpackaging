## Patterns

1. **Two different SERP worlds.** "Design agency" queries return directories (Clutch, DesignRush), not agency sites. "Custom boxes" queries return manufacturers and web-to-print stores. We sit between the two; no single site ranks for both.
2. **Web-to-print owns the low-MOQ transactional terms** (mailers, labels, pouches): Vistaprint, UPrinting, Avery. They sell from 1–100 units with instant prices. Competing head-on with MOQ 500 is weak; our angle is design + premium rigid.
3. **Rigid and collapsible are winnable.** Results are mid-size vendors (PakFactory, QinPrinting, BoxesGen, Custom Box Makers) with thin, templated product pages. Few explain board thickness, shipping flat vs assembled, or real freight savings.
4. **Canada is thin.** One templated site (thepackaging.ca) fills most "Canada" results. A real Toronto studio with design + manufacturing has room here.
5. **Buyer guides rank.** Retail Insider's "what to ask a manufacturer" guide ranks next to vendors: informational content earns spots on commercial queries.
6. **Directories are a channel, not a competitor.** For "packaging design agency Toronto" the way in is a Clutch / DesignRush profile with real reviews.
