"How much will my boxes cost?" is the first question almost every brand asks. The honest answer is that it depends, but it depends on a short, predictable list of things. Once you know them, you can steer the price before you ask for a quote.

## The five things that set the price

1. **Construction.** A folding carton or mailer is cut from one sheet and folded. A rigid box is greyboard wrapped by hand in printed paper. Rigid boxes take more material and more hands, so they cost more per unit.
2. **Size.** Bigger boxes use more board and wrap, and fewer fit on each printed sheet.
3. **Quantity.** Setup work (plates, dies, machine make-ready) is spread across the run, so the unit price falls as quantity rises.
4. **Materials.** Specialty papers, linen and leatherette cost more than standard art paper. Thicker greyboard costs more than thinner.
5. **Finishes and inserts.** Each finish, such as foil, embossing or spot UV, is an extra step. Custom inserts add material and cutting.

<Note title="Rule of thumb">
Every extra process adds cost. One finish used well usually looks more premium than three competing ones, and it costs less.
</Note>

## One-off costs vs unit costs

Some costs happen once per design:

- **Design and artwork.** At our studio the design fee is credited when we manufacture your order.
- **Tooling.** Cutting dies, foil blocks and emboss plates are made for your artwork. Reorders of the same design reuse them.
- **Samples.** A physical sample before production avoids expensive surprises.

Everything else is a unit cost that scales with quantity.

## How to lower the price without making the box look cheaper

- **Right-size the box.** A few millimetres less on each side can save board on every unit and reduce freight.
- **Use standard board thicknesses.** Our [board thickness guide](/blog/choosing-board-thickness) explains which one you actually need.
- **Pick one hero finish.** A foil logo on soft-touch lamination is a classic for a reason.
- **Order a little more.** If you will need more boxes within the year, quoting two quantities shows where the price breaks.
- **Ship flat when you can.** [Collapsible rigid boxes](/collapsible-rigid-boxes) keep the rigid feel and cut freight volume.

## Don't forget freight and duties

Box prices usually exclude shipping. Air freight is fast and priced by weight or volume, whichever is higher; sea freight is slower and cheaper per box at volume. Import duties and taxes depend on your country. Read more in our guide to [shipping rigid boxes internationally](/blog/shipping-rigid-boxes-internationally).
