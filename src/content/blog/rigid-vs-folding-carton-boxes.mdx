A rigid box and a folding carton can carry the same artwork and still feel like two different products. The difference starts with the board.

## What each one is made of

A **folding carton** is cut from a single sheet of paperboard, usually SBS or kraft between 300 and 400 gsm. It is printed, die-cut, creased and glued, then shipped flat. Tuck end boxes, sleeves and crash-lock cartons are all folding cartons.

A **rigid box** starts as greyboard, usually 1.5 to 3.0 mm thick. The board is cut and grooved to size, then wrapped by hand in a separate printed paper, linen or leatherette. The board does not flex, which is where the name comes from. Magnetic closure, lid and base, drawer and book style boxes are all rigid.

## How they feel

- A folding carton flexes when squeezed. Good print and finishes can make it look premium, but it feels light.
- A rigid box is stiff and weighty for its size. Opening one is slower, and that slowness is part of the experience.

<Note title="Rule of thumb">
If the box is part of what the customer is paying for, choose rigid. If it protects and brands the product on a shelf, a folding carton is usually enough.
</Note>

## Side by side

| | Folding carton | Rigid box |
|---|---|---|
| Board | 300–400 gsm paperboard | 1.5–3.0 mm greyboard |
| Feel | Light, flexes | Stiff, weighty |
| Unit cost | Lower | Higher |
| Ships | Flat | Assembled, or flat if collapsible |
| Best for | Retail shelves, cosmetics, supplements | Gifts, luxury retail, PR kits |

## Cost and minimums

Rigid boxes cost more per unit: more material, more hand work and more steps. Folding cartons are cheaper per unit and get cheaper still at high volumes. Our rigid boxes start at 500 per design. For a real number for your size and quantity, [ask for a quote](/get-a-quote).

## Shipping

Folding cartons ship flat. Fixed rigid boxes ship assembled, which means paying to move a lot of air. If you need the rigid feel but ship internationally, look at [collapsible rigid boxes](/collapsible-rigid-boxes): the walls fold into the base, so they ship flat too.

## Using both

Many brands combine the two:

- A folding carton for each product on the shelf, and a rigid gift box for sets and launches.
- A rigid box for the hero product, with a printed sleeve that changes per variant.
